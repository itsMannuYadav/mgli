## 🚦 1. Speed & Volume Limits (The "Safe Zones")

To protect your Local IP address from being flagged by Google, adhere to these limits:

| Metric | Safe Limit | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hourly Volume** | **150 – 200 Leads** | Mimics a high-performing human researcher. |
| **Daily Volume** | **800 – 1,200 Leads** | A full day of productivity without "Red Flags." |
| **Search Frequency** | **1 Search / 15 mins** | Use the "Cool Down" period between large Grid Searches. |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **The 24/7 Golden Rule**: Never run the bot 24 hours a day on the same IP. Let it rest for at least 8 hours every night to look like a real business.

## 🚫 2. The "NOT TO DO" List (Critical Warnings)

1.  **DO NOT** run more than one instance of the bot at the same time on the same computer (unless using proxies).
2.  **DO NOT** skip the "Cool Down" period. If you finish a huge city like Mumbai, wait at least 20 minutes before starting Delhi.
3.  **DO NOT** leave the bot running if you see repeated `[Scraper] 0 leads found` messages. This means your IP is "Sleeping"—stop the bot and wait 2-3 hours.
4.  **DO NOT** use the "Free Proxies" feature for 10,000+ leads. Free proxies are for light testing; for massive scale, use **Professional Residential Proxies.**

## 🕵️‍♂️ 3. Detecting a Block (Warning Signs)

Keep an eye on your console logs. If you see these, take action:

*   **`0 leads found. Page Title: "Are you a human?"`**: Google has triggered a CAPTCHA. Stop immediately.
*   **`net::ERR_CONNECTION_RESET`**: Your IP is being throttled or the proxy is dead.
*   **Persistent Timeouts**: Google is "Ghosting" your requests. Switch to another Wi-Fi or wait.

---

## 🛠️ 4. Recovery & Health Tips

*   **IP Rotation (WiFi Restart)**: If you ever feel you've been "flagged," simply restart your Internet Router. Most Indian ISPs give you a fresh IP address upon restart—you are now "New" again!
*   **Clean Up**: Periodically delete or move your `leads_*.csv` files to a separate "Sales" folder so the workspace stays fast and clean.
*   **Updates**: Google often changes their website layout. If the bot stops finding leads completely, it may need a CSS Selector update.
